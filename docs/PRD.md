# PRD — Feedback-to-Priorities Copilot

*(as given, kept verbatim for reference)*

**Problem:** Small-business founders and PMs have customer feedback but struggle to turn it into clear product decisions.

**Example:** Upload 100 comments → see recurring problems → inspect supporting quotes → select what to investigate next.

**The MVP would:**
- Accept a CSV of feedback.
- Group comments into editable themes.
- Show exact quotes and accurate counts.
- Suggest three product opportunities with supporting and conflicting evidence.
- Let the PM set priorities and export a decision brief.

**Proposed success targets:** Reduce analysis time by 40%; support at least 95% of factual claims with source evidence; get three pilot users to return with another batch.

**What you learn:** When AI summaries are trustworthy, how to evaluate grouping quality, and how customer evidence informs priorities.

**Note:** Dovetail already offers themes and cited customer insights. This project's focus is a simple weekly decision workflow for small teams; user testing must establish whether that is useful enough.
